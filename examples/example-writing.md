# Example: writing (Mini)

Mode: writing
Tier: Mini
Cap: 150

All names and details below are fictional.

## Source conversation (condensed)

- User writes a monthly newsletter for "Northside Climbers", a 300-member climbing club. Wants the April issue: 400 words, friendly, British English, first person plural.
- Assistant drafted an intro; user said it was "too corporate" and asked for shorter sentences. Second draft approved. Opening line kept: "Spring is here and the rock is finally dry."
- Agreed contents: trip to Stanage on 19 April, new beginners' night on Thursdays at 7pm, kit swap on 26 April.
- Rejected: a joke about falling, as the club had an injury last month. Rejected a sponsor shout-out section.
- Intro and Stanage section done. Beginners' night and kit swap sections remain. Draft is in the chat only.
