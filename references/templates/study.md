# Study / learning template

Use for exam prep, learning a subject or skill, homework help, language practice, tutoring chats.

## Emphasis

Current state (what the learner has mastered and where they struggle), Style and preferences (how they like to be taught), Open items (what to practice next).

## Brief skeleton

```
# Handoff: <subject> | study | <tier>
## Goal
<exam, skill level or project the learner is working toward, date if any>
## Decisions made
- <study plan, resources, schedule, method settled>
## Key facts
- Level: <starting level as stated>
- Syllabus / scope: <topics, chapters, exam format>
- Definitions or formulas fixed in this chat: <verbatim>
## Current state
- Understood: <topics the learner confirmed they get>
- Shaky: <topics with mistakes or confusion, what exactly went wrong>
- Not started: <topics>
## Open items / next step
1. <first topic, exercise or question to do>
## Style and preferences
- Teaching style: <examples first, step by step, quizzes, analogies>
- Pace, depth, language, tone
## Files and artifacts
- <notes, past papers, textbooks: name and relevance>
## Do not redo
- <explanation or method that did not work>: <why>
Resume: Continue from Open items. Do not re-ask answered questions. Treat Decisions made as settled unless I say otherwise.
```

## Rules

- Do not mark a topic Understood unless the learner showed it (correct answer or their own statement).
- Keep corrected misconceptions: write the wrong belief and the right one.
