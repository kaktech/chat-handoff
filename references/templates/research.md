# Research template

Use for fact-finding, literature review, comparison, due diligence, investigation.

## Emphasis

Key facts split into verified and unverified, sources, Do not redo (dead ends). Never promote an unverified claim to verified.

## Brief skeleton

```
# Handoff: <research question> | research | <tier>
## Goal
<the question to answer and how the answer will be used>
## Decisions made
- <scope, definitions, inclusion rules, source standards settled>
## Key facts
- Verified (source named): <claim> [source]
- Unverified / unconfirmed: <claim> (where it came from, what check is missing)
- Conflicting: <claim A vs claim B, sources>
## Current state
- Sources reviewed: <title/author/date/URL as given>
- Gaps: <what is still unknown>
## Open items / next step
1. <first thing to check or search>
## Style and preferences
- <citation style, depth, output format, language>
## Files and artifacts
- <notes, spreadsheets, PDFs: name and purpose>
## Do not redo
- <source or search that was empty or unreliable>: <reason>
Resume: Continue from Open items. Do not re-ask answered questions. Treat Decisions made as settled unless I say otherwise.
```

## Rules

- A claim is verified only if the chat shows the check (a source opened, a calculation done). Otherwise it is unconfirmed.
- Copy URLs, titles, dates and figures exactly. Do not add sources that were not in the chat.
- Keep each claim next to its source.
