# Writing template

Use for drafting, editing, rewriting, copywriting, emails, essays, scripts, speeches.

## Emphasis

Style and preferences (voice), audience, draft status, Do not redo (rejected phrasings and angles).

## Brief skeleton

```
# Handoff: <piece> | writing | <tier>
## Goal
<what the piece must achieve, for whom, where it will be used>
## Decisions made
- <angle, structure, title, key message settled> 
## Key facts
- Audience: <who, what they know>
- Format and length: <type, word count, deadline>
- Must include: <points, quotes, facts, verbatim>
- Must avoid: <topics, words, claims>
## Current state
- Draft status: <outline / first draft / revised v2 / final polish>
- Done: <sections finished>
- Remaining: <sections or edits left>
## Open items / next step
1. <first edit or section to write>
## Style and preferences
- Voice: <tone, person, register, with 1 to 2 short quoted phrases the user liked>
- Language and spelling variant: <e.g. British English>
- Format: <headings, bullets, paragraph length>
## Files and artifacts
- <draft name or location>: <version, what changed last>
## Do not redo
- <rejected opening, phrase, angle>: <reason the user gave>
Resume: Continue from Open items. Do not re-ask answered questions. Treat Decisions made as settled unless I say otherwise.
```

## Rules

- Do not paste the full draft. Keep the one paragraph or sentence that defines the voice if it is short, and tell the user to paste the draft again in the new chat.
- Keep the user's feedback wording ("too salesy", "shorter sentences") verbatim.
