# Business / planning template

Use for strategy, project plans, budgets, proposals, hiring, operations, launches.

## Emphasis

Key facts (stakeholders, deadlines, numbers), Decisions made, Open items with owners and dates.

## Brief skeleton

```
# Handoff: <project or decision> | business | <tier>
## Goal
<outcome, success metric, deadline>
## Decisions made
- <decision> (who decided, when, if stated)
## Key facts
- Stakeholders: <name: role, what they care about>
- Deadlines: <date: milestone>
- Numbers: <budget, price, volume, targets, exact with units and currency>
- Constraints: <legal, budget, headcount, tooling>
- Assumptions: <label each as confirmed or unconfirmed>
## Current state
- Done / In progress / Blocked (with blocker and owner)
## Open items / next step
1. <action> | owner: <name or unconfirmed> | due: <date or unconfirmed>
## Style and preferences
- <audience for outputs, format (memo, deck, table), tone, length, language>
## Files and artifacts
- <document, sheet, deck: name, version, what changed last>
## Do not redo
- <option considered and rejected>: <reason>
Resume: Continue from Open items. Do not re-ask answered questions. Treat Decisions made as settled unless I say otherwise.
```

## Rules

- Keep every number with its unit, currency and period. Never round.
- Do not infer owners or dates. Write `unconfirmed`.
- Keep confidential figures only if the user pasted them in the chat; apply the privacy pass to account numbers.
